# Niche: social, community, messaging

**Preset:** D4 · E4 · W4 — dense chat lists, lively reactions, friendly and round.
**Stakes:** trust 3/5 (privacy, safety, harassment). The risk is emotional and social more than financial.
**Session:** many short checks per day, and longer scrolling or conversation sessions in spare moments.

## The user in the moment

Seeking connection and a quick reply. Messages are real-time and emotional, so a failed or delayed send feels personal. Feeds are scrolled idly, which makes them easy to turn into compulsion. Users also need to feel safe from people they do not want to hear from.

## Structure

- **Navigation:** 4–5 tabs (for example Feed or Chats, Search or Discover, Create in the centre, Notifications, Profile). A chat list leads to a thread.
- **First screen answers:** "Who wrote to me, and what is new?" Unread conversations or the newest posts first. No full-screen promo on launch.
- **Key screens:** feed · composer · chat thread · profile · notification settings · block and report flows.
- **Chat list:** dense rows with name, last message, time and an unread marker. Pinned and muted chats are clearly different.
- **Sensitive actions:** blocking, reporting and account deletion are clear, reachable in few taps, and explain what will happen.

## Look and feel

- **Cliché to avoid:** gradient story rings and a rainbow brand colour applied to every surface.
- **Over-correction to avoid:** a grey, text-only chat with no visual difference between sent and received messages, no reactions and no personality, so the app feels like a work tool.
- **Colour:** a brand-forward accent. Sent and received bubbles differ by colour and by alignment, so meaning survives greyscale. Everything else stays neutral.
- **Type:** a legible platform font. Timestamps and secondary text stay readable, not faded to near invisible.
- **Shape:** round bubbles and avatars, medium-to-large radii on cards.
- **Motion and haptics:** playful but fast. Reactions, send animations and light haptics on react or send are welcome. Every animation can be skipped and never delays reading or replying.

## Trust signals

- Block, mute and report are easy to reach from any profile, post or message.
- Privacy controls are plain and have safe defaults.
- Moderation rules are visible, and report outcomes are acknowledged.
- State end-to-end encryption only where it is real.

## States you must design

Sending, sent, delivered, failed with retry · offline queue · typing indicator · deleted message · empty feed or empty inbox · blocked user · rate-limited action · message from a stranger (request folder) · large group with muted notifications · upload in progress.

## Refuse to build

Infinite scroll with no stopping cue; notification bait such as "someone viewed you" with no real content; engineered fear of missing out; forced app-only viewing of content that works on the web; forcing notification opt-in at sign-in; hiding block and report behind long menus.

## Learn from

- **Partiful** — Google Play Best App 2024. Borrow the light, playful tone in invitations and the quick way to create an event.
- **StoryGraph** — Apple Cultural Impact 2025. Borrow community-led discovery that centres on what people read, not on engagement metrics.

## What users complain about here

Forced app prompts and persistent banners that push web visitors into the app (reported for Reddit); forced notification opt-in at sign-in (reported for Whatnot); notification spam. These come from vendor and news summaries, so describe them as patterns.

## Accessibility specifics

- Prompt for alt text when a user posts an image, and show alt text to screen-reader users.
- Offer captions for video and respect the system reduce-motion and autoplay preferences.
- Timestamps and secondary text keep readable contrast.
- Message status (sent, failed) is not shown by colour alone.
- Reactions and swipe actions have a non-gesture alternative.

# Niche: government and civic

**Preset:** D2 · E1 · W1 — plain, quiet and official. Clear next steps, no decoration.
**Stakes:** trust 5/5 (identity, benefits, tax, safety alerts). Users cannot choose another provider, and errors can cost them money or access to services.
**Session:** infrequent, task-driven visits of 2–10 minutes. Users arrive anxious, often with a deadline.

## The user in the moment

Facing an obligation or a need: renew an ID, claim a benefit, pay tax, check an alert. They visit rarely, so they forget how it works. They may have low literacy, low vision, a second language, or little patience. Trust in the institution is a precondition; the app has to prove it quickly.

## Structure

- **Navigation:** organised by task, not by department. Few tabs. One sign-in for all services.
- **First screen answers:** "What do I need to do, and what is the status of what I started?" Open tasks and status first, then a service finder.
- **Key screens:** sign-in and identity · service finder · status tracker · wallet for credentials · notifications · help.
- **Status tracker:** shows where an application is, what happens next and when, in plain words.
- **Sensitive actions:** identity checks, payments and submissions show a clear summary and confirmation. Repeated logins are avoided.

## Look and feel

- **Cliché to avoid:** flag colours as decoration, crests everywhere and legalese in the interface.
- **Over-correction to avoid:** a trendy start-up look with playful gradients and slang, which weakens the sense that the app is official and trustworthy.
- **Colour:** an accessible, institutional palette with strong contrast. Status colours always have text.
- **Type:** a plain sans in a comfortable size, with short sentences and no jargon.
- **Shape:** small radii, clear borders, simple layouts.
- **Motion and haptics:** almost none. A confirmation haptic after a submission is enough.

## Trust signals

- Official branding and a clear privacy statement.
- Clear data use: what is collected, why, and for how long.
- Alerts come through an authoritative channel the user can recognise. One survey by a national digital service found that three in four people said they were likely to use a credential wallet if the government ran it.
- Honest explanations of failure, with the next step.

## States you must design

Identity check failed, with a recovery route · service unavailable · pending decision · offline basics (saved documents, contact info) · language switch · accessibility alternatives (phone, paper, in person) · application incomplete · session expired without losing data · alert received · document expired.

## Refuse to build

Dark patterns of any kind; collecting data beyond the stated purpose; repeated logins; vague errors such as "something went wrong"; hiding a non-digital alternative; promotional content for unrelated services.

## Learn from

- **GOV.UK app** — built around utility, ease and personalisation, and launched as a minimum viable product in June 2025 with a staged plan. Borrow the single sign-in and task-based structure, and the habit of shipping a small useful service first.
- **Watch Duty** — Apple Design Award 2025 for Social Impact. It shows fire perimeters, wind data and evacuation orders in a crisis. Borrow the clarity and speed under stress.
- **Primary: News in Depth** — Apple Design Award 2026 for Social Impact. Borrow its civic, informational focus; the Vision Pro format does not transfer.

## What users complain about here

Concerns about security and privacy, especially audit trails of digital identity use. The research found little other user complaint data. Cross-niche complaints that clearly apply: forced accounts and repeated logins, and vague errors.

## Accessibility specifics

- Meet at least the standard accessibility level and test with screen readers.
- Plain language, with multiple languages and an easy language switch.
- Keep a non-digital fallback and say how to reach it.
- Never use colour alone for status; always add text.
- Time limits are generous or adjustable, and a session timeout never discards entered data.
